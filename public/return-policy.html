Return, Refund & Quality Inspection Policy

Institutional Export Quality Assurance Guarantee

All agricultural commodities (Sugar, Soybeans, Corn, Oils, Coffee, Pulses, and Frozen Proteins) shipped by BRX Agro Global are independently assayed and certified by accredited inspection agencies (SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Cotecna) prior to vessel departure under Incoterms 2020 rules.

1. Scope of Coverage

This policy outlines the return, refund, replacement, and claim procedures for bulk commodity orders, containerized consignments (FCL), and commercial sample orders placed with BRX AGRO GLOBAL IMPORTAÇÃO E EXPORTAÇÃO LTDA.

2. Pre-Shipment Inspection & SGS Assurance

Every commercial consignment is subject to rigorous pre-shipment sampling and testing at the port of origin (Santos, Paranaguá, Rio Grande, or Salvador):

  • Phytosanitary Certification: Issued by the Brazilian Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, and Food Supply (MAPA).
  • SGS Lab Assay: Independent verification of grade, purity, moisture, polarization (ICUMSA), and microbiological standards.
  • Weight & Quantity Certificate: Issued at vessel loading / container stuffing.

3. Claims, Non-Conformity & Return Window

If any delivered lot arrives damaged during transit, manifests moisture/cargo deterioration, or deviates from agreed contract specifications upon port arrival:

  • Notice Window: Written notification must be submitted within 14 calendar days of vessel discharge / container release at the destination port.
  • Joint Inspection: A joint survey report conducted by an accredited third-party surveyor (e.g. Lloyd's Agent, SGS, or local equivalent) must be presented.
  • Defective Product Remedies: Upon verification of non-conformity, BRX Agro Global provides immediate remediation through:
    • Immediate replacement consignment dispatched under expedited logistics; or
    • Pro-rata commercial credit note or wire refund against the affected tonnage; or
    • Return and salvage coordination handled directly by our maritime logistics desk.

4. Return & Replacement Shipping Fees

  • Defective / Non-Conforming Cargo: If products are confirmed defective or failing contract specifications, BRX Agro Global covers 100% of return freight, reefer costs, and salvage logistics.
  • Restocking / Change of Mind: Due to international agricultural biosecurity and sanitary quarantine regulations, commodities that comply with certified contract specifications cannot be returned once imported through customs clearance.
